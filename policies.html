Refund & Cancellation Policy

We understand plans can change. Please contact BodyExpertX by phone or WhatsApp as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change a confirmed appointment.

1. Customer cancellation after confirmation

If a customer cancels a confirmed booking, a flat cancellation charge of ₹50 per booking will be deducted from the amount paid, including bookings made under the ₹399 Claim Massage offer. The remaining eligible amount will be refunded to the original payment method where possible.

Example: If ₹200 was paid as an advance, ₹50 is deducted as the cancellation charge and ₹150 is refunded.

2. Cancellation by BodyExpertX

If BodyExpertX cannot provide a confirmed service and the customer does not agree to a rescheduled appointment, the customer will receive a full refund of the amount paid for that booking. No ₹50 cancellation charge will apply in this situation.

3. Refund processing time

Approved refunds will be initiated to the original payment method where possible. After initiation, the time for funds to appear depends on the bank, card issuer, UPI app or payment provider. Please allow approximately 5–10 working days after the refund is processed. If the refund has not arrived after that period, contact us with the booking and transaction details.

4. How to request cancellation or refund

Contact us at WhatsApp +91 88607 42376 and share your name, booking date and payment reference if available. We will review the request and communicate the refund status.
