What students should know

Course mein kya samajhne ko milega?

Yeh learning areas course discussion aur training plan ko samajhne ke liye hain. Har topic ki exact coverage trainer aur confirmed syllabus se verify karein.

✦

Massage fundamentals

Session ki preparation, client comfort, basic body mechanics, draping aur professional communication ke principles.

◎

Pressure & technique awareness

Touch aur pressure ko client feedback ke saath adjust karna; forceful pressure ko skill samajhne ki galatfehmi se bachna.

＋

Safety & suitability

Client se relevant health information poochhne ka importance, discomfort par rukna aur jab zaroorat ho to qualified healthcare professional ki advice lena.

◇

Hygiene & boundaries

Clean hands and equipment, privacy, informed consent, respectful behaviour aur professional service boundaries.
